Privacy Policy

Video Pipeline Uploader · Last updated: November 1, 2026

Who uses this app

Video Pipeline Uploader is used only by its owner to manage the owner's YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. It has no other users.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos and thumbnails, manage playlists, read basic channel information to confirm which channel was authorized, and read YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channels.
  • Instagram: permission to read basic account information and publish Reels to the owner's professional accounts.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens and analytics reports are stored only on the owner's computer and are used only to publish the owner's videos and to review the performance of the owner's channels. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Instagram access can be revoked in Instagram settings under Apps and websites. Revoking access stops the app from using it, and the owner deletes the stored tokens.

Third-party terms

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Instagram data is subject to the Meta Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
